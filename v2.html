00How to read this

Two kinds of evidence. One map.

A confirmed indicator is an exact package-version in the scanner’s advisory snapshot. An observed staged hit is a confirmed indicator that appeared while resolving selected popular package versions. This page keeps those meanings visually separate.

The popular package matrix felt sparse because most sampled popular versions did not resolve poisoned artifacts. The atlas below fills in the confirmed campaigns without pretending those indicators were all found in React, Vue, TypeScript, or other sampled packages.

08Sources and caveats

Confirmed does not mean executed.

Confirmed indicator means exact package-version membership in the scanner snapshot. Observed staged hit means the package appeared in this experiment. Execution needs stronger local machine-state evidence.
